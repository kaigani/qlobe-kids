What changed from the first study

The earlier heightfield remains available as the flat dough study. It cannot describe a back side or preserve spherical lobes, so it is no longer proposed for Creature Studio bodies. The complementary plastic PBD study tests local dents and pulls without pretending that its fixed mesh can fuse.
