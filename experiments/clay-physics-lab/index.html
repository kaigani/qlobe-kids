What this prototype is testing

Persistence — a dent stays dented after release.

Volume — pressing moves material into a raised rim instead of deleting it.

Directness — the surface changes continuously under the pointer.

Art fit — dynamic normals, warm light, grain, and fine ridges keep the result in the claymation world.
