## Qwen extraction briefs

For complex candidates, submit the corresponding crop to local `qwen-image-layered` with `layers=2`; inspect both `layer_2` and `layer_1`, require meaningful alpha, preserve original pixels, and remove only the specified charcoal/background. Five accepted assets use Qwen output. Plain, already-separated cutter crops use deterministic edge-connected ground removal to avoid unnecessary redraw; the hollow ring uses a global sampled-ground key. Every candidate then runs the shared finalizer and magenta QA before production WebP encoding. The complete per-asset prompts, output paths, max sizes, key modes, and seeds are in [`layer-plan.json`](layer-plan.json); the 42/42 receipt is [`processing.json`](processing.json).
