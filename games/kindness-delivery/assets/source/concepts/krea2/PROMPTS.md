All four seed-42 concepts were accepted as the production style anchors. They
share the same matte paper fiber, stitched/deckled edges, soft layer depth,
muted-bright palette, and simple facial construction. The friend silhouettes,
large blank note, open flight lane, and reaction heart halo are particularly
strong.

Production changes:

- remove the unexplained concentric paper circles that Krea placed near the
  left corners; those collide with platform HUD zones and resemble controls;
- preserve more empty top-center space on select and delivery for real title/
  success text;
- keep characters out of backdrops so all three friends can reuse the same
  scenes as independent transparent sprites;

  planes/trails; those runtime actors must carry the child's actual note.
