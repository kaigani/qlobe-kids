This document preserves the earlier 31-screen audit. Current QA has 44

## Method

The first critic received three anonymously labelled A/B pairs, the original 21
real-Chrome captures, and final magenta/green asset contact sheets. Pair mapping
was withheld until initial findings were returned. After correction and reveal,
the critic rescored the packet. A separate harsh reviewer then inspected every
one of the expanded 31 canonical captures at full resolution, including all new
camera, retry, idle, mode-selection, and mode-specific end states plus all four
renamed Garden Match rewards. Any screen below 9.0 was rejected and rerun.

The two unsafe direct-target images were deliberately retained as rejected
audit controls. They are source/reference evidence, not runtime, preload,
marketing, or install candidates.

## Builder–critic loop

The first blind pass put 19 of 21 live screens at or above 9.0. It rejected:

- portrait Sequence Trail at **8.9** because the sound control collided with
  the progress badge;
- short-landscape Number Hunt at **8.9** because the basket was cropped;
- two anonymously presented direct-target compositions at **1.5** and **3.0**
  on safety grounds.

The runtime was corrected so the portrait status row reserves HUD space, the
complete basket remains inside the short viewport, the standard landscape
title has stronger hierarchy, and every success composition resolves the soft
beanbag visibly inside the basket. The reveal then established that both
direct-target failures were rejected references and that the basket-first
screens were the live candidates.

The expanded critic pass rejected the narration-only wrong-color retry at 8.6.
The runtime now places the warm correction visibly on the authored prompt
carrier, clears the consumed wrong selection, and lifts/glows the correct bag;
the refreshed state scored 9.3. A provisional #19 failure was traced to the
critic reading the PNG while the browser rerun was rewriting it. A fresh
original-resolution read of the completed file found every HUD/status element
inside frame and scored it 9.2. That artifact was withdrawn, not waived.

After the authoritative 130/130 browser rerun, the expanded critic reopened
all 31 regenerated originals. The sensitive retry, fallback, timeout, mapping,
reward, and end states remained complete. Transient viewer-side partial renders
of #29 and #30 were reopened against the unchanged source files; both originals
contained their complete controls, so no file artifact remained.

The final settled 130/130 capture set was then partitioned across three fresh
read-only critics. Their dispositions were **10/10**, **10/10**, and **11/11
PASS**. Initial viewer-side partial renders of #04, #08, #12, #16, #18, and #20
were withdrawn after the unchanged originals were reopened at native resolution.
#18 also received an independent raw-original tie-break: both HUD controls and
the complete warm-guidance carrier were present, scoring 9.3. No source-file
crop or partial-write blocker remained.
