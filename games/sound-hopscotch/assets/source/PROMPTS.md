## Qwen Image Layered extraction

All 14 cut sprites were submitted to `qwen-image-layered` at seed 42 with two
layers and the prompt below. The batch was inconsistent: the title and several
outputs retained opaque cores, while representative bunny, pad, and effect
layers had maximum alpha values of only 3–17 and were effectively empty. To
avoid mixing extraction behavior—and because the cutter masks precisely match
the approved silhouettes—the whole batch was retained as experimental
provenance but not shipped. Outputs and job IDs are in
`qwen-layered/REPORT.md`. Shipping alpha instead comes from the exact connected
component masks created by the required asset cutter and is reviewed in
`qa/alpha-contact-sheet.png`.

```text
Separate the exact single game asset as the foreground layer. Remove only the flat dark charcoal-purple background. Preserve the subject's identity, proportions, pose, cream sticker outline, colors, highlights, and every edge exactly. Do not repaint, redesign, crop, add, or remove any part of the subject. Return a clean transparent foreground suitable for a preschool tablet game.
```
