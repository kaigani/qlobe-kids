## Shared visual direction

- Standalone landscape 4:3 tablet game screens; no physical tablet, hands, room, bezel, glare, or camera perspective.
- Kawaii preschool UI for ages 3–5: soft fuzzy or clay-like blob monsters, oversized expressive eyes, blush cheeks, rounded silhouettes, friendly non-scary faces.
- Pastel lavender, mint, sky blue, coral, lemon yellow, berry pink, and deep plum outlines.
- Large touch targets, icon-led interaction, generous spacing, sparse readable copy, soft shadows, glossy rounded cards, musical sparkles and notes.
- Exact interface copy was kept intentionally minimal to support emerging readers.
