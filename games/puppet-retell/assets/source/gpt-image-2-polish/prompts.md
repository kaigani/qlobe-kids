# Puppet Tales final visual-pass prompts

Model: GPT Image 2

Style references: the finished Puppet Tales splash background and production
puppet-stage screen. All sheets use the same warm, tactile children’s
story-theater language: soft felt, painted wood, plush shapes, rounded
silhouettes, friendly faces, rich but gentle color, and no text.

## Story cards

Create one exact 3-column by 2-row contact sheet of six separate landscape
children’s story-card illustrations. Keep every panel equal in size with clean,
straight divisions and no text, letters, labels, logos, borders, or UI. Top row,
left to right: a gentle forest rescue with two plush animal friends following
tiny footprints; two plush friends on a whimsical sparkling moon adventure; a
joyful royal picnic with a tiny crown, basket, blanket, and wind-blown treats.
Bottom row, left to right: the Three Little Pigs with straw, stick, and brick
houses; Goldilocks meeting a friendly bear family in a cozy cottage; Little Red
Riding Hood safely walking a woodland path toward Grandma’s cottage. Make each
scene instantly readable for ages 4–7, non-scary, warmly lit, and compositionally
distinct. Do not place important subjects across panel dividers.

## Mode and action icons

Create one exact 3-column by 3-row contact sheet of nine separate, centered game
icons on one perfectly uniform vivid magenta chroma-key background. Leave
generous empty magenta space around every icon. No text, letters, labels,
borders, shadows touching edges, or extra objects. Row 1: a plush storybook
opening into a tiny story world; a miniature puppet theater with curtains and
two friendly puppet silhouettes; a keepsake shelf holding three saved puppet
show cards. Row 2: one friendly plush paw waving; two plush paws springing upward
with subtle motion marks; a friendly plush puppet head talking with a small
speech shape. Row 3: a thoughtful plush puppet head with one small thought cloud;
two plush paws sharing a heart-shaped hug; one golden felt star with a small
confetti burst. Use cohesive cream, teal, coral, gold, and lavender felt colors.

## Utility icons

Create one exact 3-column by 2-row contact sheet of six separate, centered game
icons on one perfectly uniform vivid magenta chroma-key background. Leave
generous empty magenta space around every icon. No text, letters, labels,
borders, shadows touching edges, or extra objects. Top row: an open plush paw
meaning no prop; a small friendly teal felt padlock meaning private on this
device; a rounded coral felt wastebasket meaning delete. Bottom row: a tiny
puppet theater with closed curtains meaning loading; a celebratory saved-show
rosette with a gold star, tiny curtains, and gentle confetti; a round teal replay
medallion with a cream play arrow. Keep every symbol simple and immediately
readable at small touch-button size.
