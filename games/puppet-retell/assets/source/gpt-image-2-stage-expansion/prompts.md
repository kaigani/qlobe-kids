# Puppet Retell stage-expansion prompts

Model: GPT Image 2, built-in image generation.

Style references: `assets/bg/puppet-theater.jpg` and
`assets/bg/sunny-meadow.jpg`.

All three prompts requested a straight-on 4:3 children’s puppet-stage
background in the established handcrafted plush style: layered wool felt,
visible stitching, softly painted wood, rounded forms, curtains at the far
edges, and a clear central/lower performance area. They explicitly excluded
characters, animals, people, text, logos, UI, photorealism, scary elements,
and foreground objects that could obscure the puppets.

## Enchanted Castle

A welcoming royal castle courtyard with lavender towers, golden banners,
jewel-tone flowers, warm magical late-afternoon light, and berry-red curtains.

## Moon Adventure

A whimsical felt moon surface with stitched craters, a rounded rocket parked
far to one side, tiny golden stars, a soft blue Earth, indigo sky, and teal
curtains.

## Forest Cottage

An inviting woodland clearing with a small cottage well back to one side, a
winding path, rounded trees, mushrooms and wildflowers around the perimeter,
dappled morning light, and warm orange curtains.
