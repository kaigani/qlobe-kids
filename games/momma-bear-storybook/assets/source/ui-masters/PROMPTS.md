## Extraction ledger

The original eleven Qwen Image Layered follow-on jobs remain recorded in
`qwen-layer-jobs.json`, but were cancelled before execution after a direct
chroma-key comparison. The ten used controls were extracted from the accepted
GPT pixels with `tools/cutout-to-webp.mjs` (`#f80488`, similarity `0.20`, blend
`0.05`, three alpha erosions, 12–20 px trim padding) and accepted after dark-
background edge and phone-size icon review. `button-lamp` is intentionally
unused. This avoids a generative redraw of exact title lettering and icons;
Qwen Layered remains the chosen upscaling/extraction path for the larger actor
and prop art.

Contact-sheet boundary review found neighboring-cell slivers on four direct
extracts. The accepted runtime replacements were isolated before chroma keying:

- `word-tile-done-isolated.png`: crop `358×512+26+0` from its raw cell.
- `act-tab-isolated.png`: crop `320×512+0+0` from its raw cell.
- `button-again-isolated.png`: crop `344×512+40+0` from its raw cell.
- `page-turn-isolated.png`: crop `240×512+0+0` from its raw cell.

Each isolated crop was processed with the same `#f80488`, similarity `0.20`,
blend `0.05`, three-alpha-erosion recipe, then accepted on a near-black matte.
`page-stitch` showed no neighboring fragment and remains the direct extraction.
