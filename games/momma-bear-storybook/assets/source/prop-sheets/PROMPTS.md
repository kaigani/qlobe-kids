## Final extraction ledger

The 24 optional Qwen Image Layered follow-on jobs are recorded in
`qwen-layer-jobs.json` and were cancelled before execution. The accepted GPT
Image 2 pixels retained the strongest identity at the 96–300 px runtime scale;
Qwen Layered remains in the actual production chain for all four pose actors.

Runtime props were keyed with `tools/cutout-to-webp.mjs` using sampled magenta
`#f80488`, similarity `0.20`, blend `0.05`, two to three alpha erosions, 16–18
px trim padding, and WebP q91–93. Dark-matte contact review found five
neighboring-cell fragments, so these crops were isolated and reprocessed:

- `water-ribbon`: `344×512+40+0`
- `silver-horse`: `368×512+0+0`
- `vellum-trail`: `350×512+0+0`
- `gold-apples`: `260×512+0+0`
- `village-friends`: `342×512+42+0`

The corrected sources are retained in `assets/source/prop-isolated/`. All 24
runtime silhouettes passed the final dark-background and small-size review;
the accepted correction contact is
`qa-shots/momma-bear-storybook/props-isolated-dark-contact-v1.png`.
