# The Little Mill and the Sea

A simple early-reader story about Pip, a kind old man, and a little
mill with a very big secret.

## Beginning

### Page 1

Pip has one warm bun.

(Visual: Pip standing outside holding a single round, steaming bun,
smiling.)

### Page 2

Pip shares half with a kind old man.

(Visual: Pip breaking the bun in two and handing one half to a
smiling old man leaning on a walking stick.)

## Middle

### Page 3

The man gives Pip a magic mill. "Say 'Grind!' to start, and 'Rest!'
to stop," he says.

(Visual: The old man handing Pip a small wooden mill that glows
softly. Pip listening closely with wide, curious eyes.)

### Page 4

Pip and the mill sail on a ship. "Grind! Make salt!" says the
captain.

(Visual: Pip standing on the deck of a small ship holding the mill.
A captain pointing at it eagerly, waves and sky in the background.)

## Ending

### Page 5

Whirr, whirr! The captain forgets to say "Rest!" Salt spills into the
sea!

(Visual: The mill spinning fast, a big stream of sparkly white salt
pouring out and over the side of the ship into the water. The captain
looking worried.)

### Page 6

Pip shouts, "Rest!" The mill stops. The sea sparkles with salt.

(Visual: Pip with hands cupped around their mouth, shouting. The mill
sitting still and calm. The whole sea around the ship glittering and
sparkling.)

## Story message

Pip shared, remembered the words, and saved the sea.
