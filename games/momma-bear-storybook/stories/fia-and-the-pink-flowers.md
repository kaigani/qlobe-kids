# Fia and the Pink Flowers

A simple early-reader story about a girl named Fia, her pot of pink
flowers, a kind Wood Queen, and one very silly Magic Cabbage.

## Beginning

### Page 1

Fia loves her pot of pink flowers.

(Visual: Fia smiling next to a clay pot filled with pink flowers on a
sunny windowsill.)

### Page 2

One windy day, the pot falls. The pink flowers fall out!

(Visual: Wind blowing, the pot tipped over on its side, pink flower
petals scattered on the grass. Fia's mouth open in surprise.)

## Middle

### Page 3

"Take this Magic Cabbage," says the kind Wood Queen.

(Visual: A gentle, leafy Wood Queen handing Fia a round green cabbage
with a friendly, funny face. Fia reaching out to take it.)

### Page 4

The Magic Cabbage giggles, "Look in the grass!"

(Visual: Fia holding the cabbage close to her ear. The cabbage's face
scrunched up in a giggle, one leaf pointing toward the grass.)

## Ending

### Page 5

Fia looks in the grass. She finds her pink flowers!

(Visual: Fia kneeling in the grass, gently gathering pink flowers
into her arms. The Magic Cabbage watching happily beside her.)

### Page 6

She puts them back in the pot. Pop, pop, pop! The pinks bloom.

(Visual: Fia setting the flowers into the same tipped-over pot, now
upright. The flowers popping open into bloom, petals and sparkles in
the air.)

## Story message

Fia looked for her flowers, cared for them, and they grew.
