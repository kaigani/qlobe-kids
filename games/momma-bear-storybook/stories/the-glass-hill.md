# The Glass Hill

A simple early-reader story about Ash, three magic horses, and a tall,
shiny glass hill.

## Beginning

### Page 1

Ash keeps watch by the hay. Something eats it every night!

(Visual: Ash sitting by a lantern next to a big haystack at night,
looking around with curious, alert eyes.)

### Page 2

Boom! A bright copper horse comes. Ash catches it!

(Visual: A glowing copper-colored horse galloping up to the haystack.
Ash reaching out and holding its rope, both looking surprised and
excited.)

## Middle

### Page 3

A silver horse comes. Then a gold horse comes too!

(Visual: Three horses standing together — copper, silver, and gold,
each shimmering. Ash standing proudly between them, holding all three
ropes.)

### Page 4

Ash rides the horses up a tall glass hill.

(Visual: A steep, sparkling glass hill glinting in the sun. Ash riding
the gold horse up the slope, with the silver and copper horses
climbing close behind, sparks flying from their hooves.)

## Ending

### Page 5

At the top, three gold apples shine!

(Visual: The very top of the glass hill. A small tree holds three
glowing golden apples, with the whole land visible far below.)

### Page 6

Ash picks the apples and brings them home to share.

(Visual: Ash walking down a grassy path, holding three golden apples.
Friends and family wave happily from a cozy house up ahead.)

## Story message

Ash watched closely, worked hard, and shared.
