# GPT Image 2 production prompts — My Awesome Day

The accepted masters in this folder are generated through Codex built-in image
generation on the requested GPT Image 2 path. The supplied concept mockups are
style/composition references only; runtime art is newly authored. Every prompt
uses the canonical **Puppet / Cozy felt fabric** art world.

Shared style anchor for all game-interior assets:

> Premium handcrafted preschool quiet-book art built from wool felt, fuzzy
> fleece, yarn, embroidery, blanket stitching, soft stuffing, and subtle warm
> contact shadows. Visible fibers and tiny handmade imperfections. One warm
> upper-left studio light. Palette: warm cream, cobalt blue, leaf green, golden
> yellow, tomato red, lavender, orange, and dark plum. Friendly tactile forms,
> not vector art, not glossy plastic, not papercraft, not clay, not flat UI.
