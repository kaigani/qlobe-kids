speech.js test

window.speechSynthesis is monkey-patched with a fake engine before speech.js is imported (a dynamic import(), so the patch lands before the module's top-level pickVoice() runs) — deterministic and silent, instead of racing a real system voice engine that may not even be installed in a headless tab. The behaviour under test: the unlock latch, the iOS-GC utterance-ref keepalive, cancel-on-first-fragment-only in a sequence, and the bounded safety timeout that must resolve `speak()` even if the engine never fires its events.
